THE ROOM

Built for the set you don't do anywhere else.

The Wyckoff Loft is a tiny, performer-run comedy room above Wyckoff Avenue. We do two or three nights a month: stand-up, story shows and the occasional odd format that needs a forgiving crowd.

No drink minimum. No velvet rope. One narrow flight of stairs, so the room is unfortunately not wheelchair accessible. Ages 21+ unless the listing says otherwise.
